## Core Features & Workflow

### 1. Submit Personal Loan Application
The platform allows administrators and authorized users to initiate and submit new personal loan applications on behalf of applicants.
- **Key Fields Required:** Borrower details, requested loan amount, loan tenure, income verification, and employment information.
- **Action:** Navigate to the "Loan Applications" section within the dashboard, click **New Application**, fill out the required forms, and submit for review.

### 2. Search for Loan Details
Easily locate, track, and review existing loan files within the system.
- **Search Criteria:** Search by applicant name, application ID, phone number, or current loan status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
- **Action:** Use the global search bar or navigate to the **Loan Details** tab to filter and view comprehensive application histories and statuses.

### 3. Complete KYC Registration
Ensure regulatory compliance by verifying the identity of applicants through the Know Your Customer (KYC) module.
- **Required Documents:** Government-issued identification (ID/Passport), proof of address, and biometric or photo verification if applicable.
- **Action:** Open the specific loan profile, go to the **KYC Verification** tab, upload or verify the submitted documents, and update the status to "Verified".
